# Visual+ English Port

English (USA) port of **Pokémon Visual+ v1.0.0** — a visual enhancement mod
for Pokémon HeartGold & SoulSilver, originally created for the Korean
HeartGold by **민트초크 (MinT ChoK)** with battle artwork by **Young**.

> **This project does not distribute ROMs.** Users must supply a legally
> obtained ROM dump from a game they own where permitted by applicable law.

## What it changes

1. 🎨 **New battle backgrounds** — vivid redrawn battle scenes
2. 🎥 **Enhanced 3D camera** — more dramatic overworld perspective
3. ⚡ **Faster HP bar** — HP drains ~7–8× faster in battle
